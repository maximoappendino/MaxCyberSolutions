§ 03  /  Questions

Answered, honestly.

If yours isn't here, book a call — I'll answer it in person.

Do I need any technical knowledge to manage my store?

None at all. If you can use a messaging app and fill out a form, you can run your Dashboard. I handle the code; you handle your business. Setup, updates, and fixes are all on my side.

Which payment methods does MercadoPago support?

Credit and debit cards from all major banks, cash payments at Rapipago and PagoFácil, bank transfers, and MercadoPago digital wallets. All of it integrated at checkout with no extra configuration required from you.

How does shipping work?

MercadoEnvíos is built into checkout. Customers see live shipping quotes before paying. Once the order is placed, you print the label from your Dashboard and drop the package at the nearest post office or pickup point — tracking is automatic from there.

Can I have more than one store?

Yes. The Pro plan supports 2 websites and Ultra supports 5. Each store has its own independent catalog, orders, customers, and analytics — managed from the same Dashboard.

How long does it take to launch?

Most stores go live 3 to 7 days after the first call, depending on how large the catalog is. Simple stores with fewer than 30 products can often be live within 24 hours.

What happens when I need to update prices or add products?

You do it yourself in the Dashboard, in real time. No waiting, no tickets, no email threads. Changes to prices, stock, images, and descriptions appear on your storefront immediately after saving.

Is there a free trial?

There's no free trial, but there is a free conversation over WhatsApp where I show you a live demo of the kind of store that fits your business. If it's not what you need after seeing it, you've lost nothing.

What does Technical Support include?

Direct access to me — not a ticket system, not a chatbot, not an outsourced team. If something breaks, I fix it. If you need a feature, I add it to the roadmap and tell you honestly when it will land.
